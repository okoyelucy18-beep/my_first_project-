Charism & Holy Labor

About Us

Living behind the quiet grilles of the strict papal enclosure, the Carmelite Nuns devote themselves to interior stillness, manual labor, and ceaseless adoration on behalf of all souls.

local_fire_department

Unceasing Intercession

Consecrated to the contemplative mysticism of St. John of the Cross and St. Teresa of Ávila, the nuns stand as a powerhouse of uninterrupted prayer, bearing before the Lord the trials of the Catholic Church, the peace of Nigeria, and the burdens of humanity.

Pure Altar Breads

In prayerful silence, the sisters bake and seal pure wheat Eucharistic hosts according to strict liturgical canons. Their altar breads nourish holy Masses in cathedral altars, rural chapels, and mission churches across the dioceses of eastern Nigeria.

Perpetual Adoration

A serene consecrated Adoration Chapel welcomes pilgrims, priests, and seekers daily. Exposed before the Most Holy Sacrament, the sanctuary radiates deep, reverent quietude, offering spiritual rest and solace away from the noise of the marketplace.

Location & Vicinity

A Sanctuary Enfolded by Fragrant Gardens and Prayerful Groves

Situated within an exceptionally serene monastic territory in Anambra State, the monastery grounds are physically and sonically buffered from motorways and commerce. Shaded footpaths guide retreatants through the outdoor Stations of the Cross, bougainvillea archways, and olive-green palms.

Sanctuary Etiquette

Do’s & Don’ts for Visitors

The cloister is an enclosure of mystical silence and contemplative virginity. We kindly ask all pilgrims and friends to honor these rules of peace.

check

Reverence to Uphold

Honoring the Sacred Enclosure
  • volume_off
    Maintain Profound Silence

    Observe absolute stillness in the Adoration Chapel and surrounding garden cloisters at all times so that private prayer remains undisturbed.

  • mail
    Bring Prayers to the Parlor

    You are encouraged to deposit your written prayer intentions and Mass stipend requests at the monastery reception parlor for the intercession of the sisters.

  • apparel
    Dress with Modesty and Respect

    Wear clothing that covers shoulders and knees when visiting the sacred chapel, prayer gardens, or reception areas.

block

Boundaries to Respect

Preserving Cloistered Solitude
  • do_not_disturb_on
    Do Not Breach the Cloister Grille

    Never attempt to cross into the enclosed sisters’ living quarters, private kitchen gardens, or inner choir enclosures.

  • no_photography
    No Mobile Phones or Camera Flash

    Strictly switch mobile devices to silent. Photography, videography, or flash use inside the Adoration Chapel is strictly prohibited.

  • delete_forever
    No Litter or Single-Use Plastics

    Do not leave food remains, plastic water bottles, or debris outside designated bins in the visitor reception area.

Pilgrim Journey

Directions & Travel Guide

The monastery is situated in an easily accessible haven within the pastoral belt of Anambra State, welcoming day pilgrims, prayer groups, and quiet travelers.

directions_car From Awka Central (Capital City)

Drive along the Awka-Ekwulobia or Awka-Enugu artery towards the marked monastic access junction. Average driving time is 35–45 minutes under standard morning conditions.

near_me From Onitsha Metropolitan Axis

Connect through the Oba or Nnewi arterial bypass towards the central diocesan road. Distinct brown monastic signs guide visitors directly toward the quiet sanctuary gates.

directions_bus Public Transit & Regional Shuttles

Express commuter buses and shuttles bound for Ekwulobia or Awka pass directly through the main crossroads. Disembark at Carmelite Junction, where designated transport waits.

Carmelite Monastery Gate Anambra, NG

Clear signposts installed at every major fork along the paved diocesan approach road.

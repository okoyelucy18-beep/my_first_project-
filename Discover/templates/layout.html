volume_off The Great Silence (Silentium Magnum)

The Architecture of Stillness

Silence is not merely the absence of sound, but the presence of attentiveness to God. Monasteries observe two liturgical spheres of quietude:

Daytime Cloistral Silence 06:00 – 20:30

Low voices permitted strictly in guest hospitium, refectory porches, and pilgrimage chapels.

Grand Silence 21:00 – 06:00

Absolute silence across the entire monastic territory. No speech, no devices; solitary communion with God.

Opus Dei • The Work of God

The Rhythm of the Hours

Monastic day is woven with seven canonical pauses, consecrating every turn of daylight and night to praise and contemplation.

Visitors residing in the hospitium are warmly encouraged to enter the cloister chapel quietly before the choir bell rings. Breviaries and Latin/English psalters are placed at the chapel entrance for all faithful.

“Seven times a day have I praised Thee, because of the judgments of Thy justice.”

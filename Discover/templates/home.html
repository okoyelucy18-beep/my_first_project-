Sacred Etiquette

Do’s & Don’ts for Retreatants & Visitors

The monastery is neither a tourist resort nor a public park; it is a cloistered sanctuary of perpetual prayer. Your strict cooperation honors the brethren.

check_circle

Reverent Observances (DO)

check

Preserve Continuous Quietude

Observe the monastic atmosphere of silence and recollected peace across the courtyards, cloisters, and walking gardens.

check

Reverent Participation in Liturgy

Join the monks in the chapel for Holy Mass and the canonical hours. Enter promptly and bow towards the tabernacle upon crossing the nave.

check

Power Off Electronic Devices

Switch off mobile phones or engage silent flight mode immediately upon entering the sanctuary gates. Emergency contacts are handled by the Guestmaster.

check

Modest Pilgrimage Attire

Wear modest, understated clothing covering shoulders and knees out of respect for sacred worship and consecrated monks.

block

Strict Prohibitions (DON’T)

close

No Pets, Alcohol, or Audio Systems

Do not bring animals, alcoholic beverages, smoking materials, or bluetooth speakers into retreat accommodations or forest grounds.

close

No Candid Photography of Brothers

Never photograph, film, or livestream the monks in prayer, labor, or walking without express, written clearance from the Father Guestmaster.

close

No Trespassing Into Enclosed Work Areas

Do not wander into active agrarian fields, livestock quarters, or candle fabrication workshops while heavy monastic labor is underway.

close

No Social Loud Gatherings

Avoid social chatting in corridors, common rooms, or chapel porches. Speaking is strictly reserved for the Guest Parlor during scheduled hours.

night_shelter Hospitium Sanctum

Retreat Lodging & Hermitage Stays

The monastery guest wing offers single austere cells equipped with a writing desk, mosquito netting, private washroom, and scripture Bible. All meals (breakfast, lunch, and supper) are wholesome, quiet, and prepared with monastery farm produce.

Detailed Travel Directions to Holy Cross

Nteje is nestled conveniently off the newly upgraded expressway. Whether arriving by personal vehicle, charter, or public transit, follow these exact monastic route checkpoints.

directions_car

From Onitsha Commercial City

~20 Minutes

Head east out of Onitsha via the Onitsha-Enugu Expressway passing Borromeo Roundabout towards the Awkuzu / Nteje bypass. Exit at Nteje junction near the Oyi LGA Secretariat, and proceed along the paved monastery access road directly to the outer gate.

explore

From Awka Capital City

~25 Minutes

Travel west along the Awka–Enugu-Onitsha Expressway towards Onitsha. Turn off into the marked Nteje junction before reaching the tollgate area. Clear ecclesiastical signboards lead directly through the peaceful village grove to the monastery.

commute

Public Shuttle & Transit Tip

Economy Route

Board any commercial vehicle or shuttle bound for Awkuzu/Nteje from Onitsha Upper Iweka or Awka Unizik junction. Disembark at Nteje Monastery Junction where local registered motorcycle (Okada) riders can escort you safely past the outer enclosure.
